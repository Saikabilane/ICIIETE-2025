# International Conference on Intelligent Innovations in Engineering and Technology (ICIIET)

Official website for the **International Conference on Intelligent Innovations in Engineering and Technology (ICIIET)**, organized by the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE), PSG College of Technology, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India, in collaboration with PSG Centre for Academic Research and Excellence (PSG CARE).

## 🌐 Live Website
The website is hosted live on GitHub Pages:
**[https://saikabilane.github.io/ICIIETE-2025/](https://saikabilane.github.io/ICIIETE-2025/)**

---

## 📑 Pages Included

* **`index.html`** — Home, About Us, Call for Papers, Tracks (1 to 6), Important Dates, Committee Overview, Registration, and Contact Details with Map.
* **`organizing_committee.html`** — Chief Patron, Patron, Co-Patron, General Chair, and Organizing Chairs.
* **`advisory_committee.html`** — Advisory Committee members from premier institutions worldwide.
* **`technical_committee.html`** — Technical Program Committee members from leading universities and research organizations.
* **`program_committee.html`** — Complete Program Committee grouped by roles (Publication Chair, Program Chair, Finance Chair, Hospitality & Logistics, Publicity / Virtual Platform, Preconference Workshop).
